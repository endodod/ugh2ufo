# Name Candidates

## The whole arc in one line
Names that tell you the game goes from cave to spaceship.

- **Ugh to UFO** - From the first grunt to the first abduction, in about 25 minutes.
- **Sticks to Saucers** - You start with sticks. Things escalate.
- **Clubs to Cosmos** - Same idea, a bit grander, a bit less funny.
- **Nine Ages Later** - Sounds like a title card. Works as a punchline after a disaster.
- **From Rocks to Rockets** - Clear and friendly, but generic enough to get lost in search.

## Deadpan
Dry names that sound like they're shrugging.

- **Progress, Probably** - The official motto of every civilization ever.
- **Barely Civilized** - Honest about your empire. (Taken, see below.)
- **Mostly Fine Empire** - For when the capital is on fire but morale is up.
- **Emperor of Whatever** - You rule. Over what is up for debate.
- **Civilization-ish** - Too close to a famous franchise to be safe.

## The alien deadline
Names built around the invasion at the end.

- **Prepare to Be Probed** - Funny, but the joke is the whole game in a way that wears thin.
- **Last Turn Before Contact** - Tense, a bit too serious for the tone.
- **They're Coming, Act Normal** - Good as a tagline, clunky as a title.
- **Saucer Season** - Short and catchy, but undersells the history part.

## Paperwork
Leaning into the "one big decision per turn" and bureaucracy jokes.

- **Ministry of Ages** - Sounds like a real department. Sounds like it has forms.
- **Decree Fatigue** - You sign one thing a turn and still regret it.
- **Office of Inevitable Progress** - Too long, but great as an in-game faction.

## Wordplay
- **Rock Paper Saucer** - Stone Age, bureaucracy, aliens. That's the game.
- **Hold My Empire** - Reckless energy, fits the random events.

---

## Top 3

### 1. Ugh to UFO (recommended)
- **Memorable:** three short words, the start and end of the game, and it's fun to say out loud.
- **Searchable:** "ugh to ufo" is a strange enough phrase that the game would own the search results quickly.
- **Tone:** "Ugh" is both a caveman grunt and how you'll feel when the aliens show up. That double reading matches the dry humor.
- **Conflicts:** a web search for "Ugh to UFO" turned up no game, app, or product with that name, only unrelated UFO-themed games.
- **Risk:** a few people may hear "ugh" as a negative word. In practice the logo and tagline make the joke clear.

### 2. Rock Paper Saucer
- **Memorable:** it takes a phrase everyone knows and swaps in one word.
- **Searchable:** weaker. Search engines tend to "correct" it to rock paper scissors, which could bury the game.
- **Conflicts:** a web search found no game with this exact name. There are many rock-paper-scissors variants nearby.

### 3. Sticks to Saucers
- **Memorable:** alliterative and it shows the arc, but softer than "Ugh to UFO".
- **Searchable:** good, the phrase is unusual.
- **Conflicts:** a web search found no game with this name.

### Ruled out after searching
- **Barely Civilized** is already used by an indie open-world survival game on IndieDB (ArTchie Studios, in alpha). Too close.

_Note: these were quick web searches, not a trademark search. Before launch, check itch.io, Steam, the app stores, and a trademark database for the final pick._
